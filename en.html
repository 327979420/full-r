02 · REBATE

What is a rebate?

Part of the trading cost charged by a broker may be paid back as commission. That returnable portion can go to the trader or to the IB who introduced and supports the client, rather than being withheld by an intermediary.

STEP 01

Trade as usual

Same account, platform and order process.

STEP 02

Eligible rebate is generated

Calculated from account group and eligible trading records.

STEP 03

Rebate comes back to you

We do not take an extra cut from the returnable portion.

08 · FAQ

FAQ

The most common questions, kept short.

Does the rebate change my spread or trading conditions?

Under the current Max Rebate plan, the rebate service does not add a spread markup. Actual conditions still depend on the platform, account group and market.

What does “100% rebate” mean?

100% means that after we actually receive the broker rebate, we return 100% of the eligible client rebate portion to the client. The spread is a fixed trading cost and cannot be refunded.

Can an existing TMGM account apply?

You can request a review. Attribution or group changes remain subject to platform rules.

When is the rebate credited?

Under normal circumstances, the rebate is credited within one business day. For example, positions closed on Monday can be credited on Tuesday. Exceptional cases may take longer.

Will you ask for my trading password?

No. Never send passwords, verification codes, card passwords or identity-document images.

Does a rebate guarantee profit?

No. A rebate may reduce eligible costs but does not reduce market risk or guarantee profit.

What permissions does an agent receive?

Once agent access is activated, the agent receives a dedicated account-opening link and invitation code for business development. Agent rebates are determined by the client account group, trading instrument and eligible trading volume. Client account profits or losses do not affect agent income.
