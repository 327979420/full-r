Why rebates matter more for EAs

The more lots a strategy trades, the more the per-lot rebate adds up. On a Max Rebate TMGM Standard (STD) account, gold earns USD 20 per standard lot, so 100 closed lots of gold in a month is USD 2,000.

  • EA trades earn rebates exactly like manual trades, on eligible closed standard lots.
  • Low-spread accounts charge commission. On the current Max Rebate table, RAW07 gold has a reference commission of USD 5 per lot and a rebate of USD 2.
  • Rebates are settled daily and paid to the trading account the next day.

Frequent trading also adds spread, commission and slippage on every trade. Work out the net cost first, as in forex trading costs. A rebate lowers costs you have already paid; it does not turn a losing strategy into a winning one.

Before you run an EA

  1. Test on demo first. TMGM demo accounts last 30 days, and you can open a new one when it expires.
  2. Pick the account by trading frequency. Compare spread, commission and rebate for the way the EA trades.
  3. Watch news and market opens. Spreads can widen and fills can slip; see why forex spreads widen.
  4. Size positions with the contract specification. See TMGM lot size and pip value.
  5. Use only EAs from a clear source. Avoid cracked or unknown programs, and never give your trading password to someone else to run it for you.

Frequently asked questions

Does TMGM allow Expert Advisors?

Yes. TMGM's MT4 page says the platform supports Expert Advisors for automated trading, and its account types page lists both EDGE and CLASSIC as EA compatible.

Does TMGM allow scalping?

Yes. TMGM's MT4 page says there are no scalping restrictions.

Is the TMGM VPS free?

Free if you meet the conditions: existing clients need 7 lots traded before the month-end review, and new clients need a deposit of USD 3,000 for Basic or USD 20,000 for High Frequency. The free offer is for a Basic VPS. Checked 2026-11-24; TMGM's page applies.

Do trades placed by an EA earn rebates?

Yes. EA trades are counted the same way as manual trades, by eligible closed standard lots. Using an EA does not change the rebate.

Will TMGM suddenly restrict my account for using an EA?

In normal use, usually not. If TMGM considers a trading pattern risky, it generally emails first and explains what to adjust. If nothing changes after the reminder, TMGM may act under the client agreement.
