TMGM MT4/MT5 login not working? Three causes to check in order

A failed first login almost always comes down to the server, the password or an account that is not active yet. Work through them in this order before contacting support.

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Why can't I log in to TMGM MT4 or MT5?

In first-login cases Max Rebate has helped with, roughly six in ten were the wrong server, three in ten a password problem, and one in ten a trading account that was not yet active.

  • Pick the exact server shown for your account on the Client Portal home page.
  • Log in with the trading account number and trading password, typed by hand.
  • If both are right and it still fails, ask TMGM to check that the account is active.

Stay safe: never send your trading password or a verification code to anyone, including people who say they are support staff or an IB. To get help, share only your trading account number and the error message.

Cause 1: the wrong server

TMGM's own MT4 guide warns that choosing the wrong server makes your login details unrecognised, and MT4 shows "Invalid Account" in the bottom-right corner. It usually goes wrong in one of three ways:

  • Live or demo: a live account needs a live server and a demo account needs a demo server.
  • Region: servers with AU in the name are for clients of TMGM's Australian entity. Clients elsewhere use the server their account was opened on.
  • Number: several servers can share the same prefix. The full name must match your account exactly.

TMGM's help centre says the server name for each trading account is shown on the home page of the Client Portal. In the MT4/MT5 server search, type "TradeMax" or "TMGM" and choose that exact name.

On MT4 for Mac, if the servers do not appear, TMGM suggests File → Open an Account → Add new broker, typing "TradeMax" and clicking Scan.

Cause 2: the password

MT4/MT5 needs the trading account number and the trading password, not your email address and Client Portal password.

  • Type it by hand: copying from an email often adds a space or drops a character.
  • Use the number: the MT4/MT5 login is the numeric trading account number, not your registration email.
  • Two passwords: TMGM's FAQ says the Client Portal password is different from the trading account password. Some older TMGM guides describe them as the same, so check which one your account uses.
  • Forgotten: reset the trading account password in the Client Portal, or ask TMGM support if you cannot find the option.

Cause 3: the account is not active yet

If the server and password are both confirmed and the login still fails, the trading account has usually not been activated yet. Changing settings on your side will not fix this; TMGM has to check it.

  • Contact TMGM live chat, or email support@tmgm.com with your trading account number and the error you see.
  • If you opened the account through a Max Rebate link, you can also send the account number and the problem through the contact page, and Max Rebate will pass it on.

Checklist

  1. Open the Client Portal and note the trading account number and the full server name.
  2. In MT4/MT5, search the server list and pick the name that matches exactly, live or demo.
  3. Log in with the trading account number and the trading password, typed by hand.
  4. Still failing? Reset the trading password in the Client Portal and try again.
  5. Still failing? Contact TMGM support and say the account may not be active yet.

If you are logged in but quotes freeze or keep loading, TMGM suggests right-clicking the connection bars and choosing Rescan Servers, and checking that firewall or antivirus software is not blocking the platform.

Frequently asked questions

What does "Invalid Account" mean in TMGM MT4?

MT4 could not match your login details on the selected server. TMGM says the usual cause is choosing the wrong server. Check the server name shown on the Client Portal home page, then try the trading password again.

Do I log in to MT4/MT5 with my email or my account number?

With the trading account number, the string of digits shown in the Client Portal. Your email address is for the Client Portal, not for MT4/MT5.

Is the Client Portal password the same as the MT4/MT5 password?

TMGM's FAQ says they are different. Use the trading account password in MT4/MT5, and reset it in the Client Portal if you have forgotten it.

Why can I see my account but not place trades?

You may have logged in with the investor password. TMGM's help centre explains that it gives read-only access: you can view trading activity but cannot place or manage trades. Log in again with the trading password.

How long does account activation take?

There is no fixed time because TMGM checks it manually. Contact live chat or email support with your trading account number and follow their reply.
