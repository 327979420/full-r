TMGM trading hours: gold, silver, oil and forex in UTC

TMGM publishes its hours in server time. This guide converts them to UTC and to UTC+8 (Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Hong Kong, Beijing), and shows when US daylight saving moves them by an hour.

Last updated: 2026-11-25

When does TMGM gold open and close?

Now (standard time, since 2 November 2026), TMGM gold opens on Sunday at 23:01 UTC, which is Monday 07:01 in UTC+8. From Monday to Thursday it pauses at 21:59 UTC and reopens at 23:01 UTC, and it closes for the week on Friday at 21:55 UTC.

  • TMGM server time is GMT+2, or GMT+3 during US daylight saving.
  • From 14 March 2027, US daylight saving starts and every time below moves one hour earlier in UTC.

When daylight saving changes the times

TMGM says its server time and charts are set to GMT+2, or GMT+3 during daylight saving. The switch follows US daylight saving.

  • US daylight saving ended on Sunday 1 November 2026, so standard time applies from the Monday 2 November open. Use the table above.
  • US daylight saving starts on Sunday 14 March 2027. From then on, every UTC and UTC+8 time in the table is one hour earlier; for example, gold opens on Sunday at 22:01 UTC.

Server-time sessions stay the same all year; only your local times move. In the days around a switch, read the server time in MT4/MT5 and convert it again.

Leverage before closes and major news

Before the daily close and around major releases such as US payrolls, CPI and rate decisions, the platform may temporarily limit or reduce leverage. Liquidity is thinnest then and prices can gap, so the same position may need more margin. Changes are announced in advance; check the day's notice before holding positions through these times.

Spreads also tend to widen at the same moments. See why forex spreads widen for the causes and what to check.

Check server time in MT4/MT5

  1. Open the Market Watch window; the current server time is shown at the top.
  2. Compare it with UTC: two hours ahead means standard time (GMT+2), three hours ahead means daylight saving (GMT+3).
  3. Right-click a symbol and open Specification to see its sessions, which are also listed in server time.

Frequently asked questions

What time does TMGM gold open on Monday?

In standard time, which applies now, gold opens on Sunday at 23:01 UTC, which is Monday 07:01 in UTC+8. During US daylight saving it opens an hour earlier, at 22:01 UTC.

What time zone is TMGM server time?

TMGM's trading hours page says server time and charts are GMT+2, or GMT+3 during daylight saving. The switch follows US daylight saving.

Why does TMGM gold stop trading for an hour every day?

The gold session in TMGM's schedule ends at 23:59 server time and restarts at 01:01, so there is a daily pause of about an hour. In standard time that is 21:59 to 23:01 UTC.

Do trading hours change when daylight saving ends?

Not in server time, which moves with US daylight saving. In UTC and in places without daylight saving, such as UTC+8, the open and close are one hour later in standard time and one hour earlier once US daylight saving starts.

Where can I find holiday trading hours?

Holiday sessions are set separately and announced by TMGM before each holiday. This page converts normal trading days only.
