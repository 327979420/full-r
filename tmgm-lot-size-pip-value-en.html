TMGM lot size and pip value: forex, gold, oil and US30

One lot is a different amount for each instrument. This guide uses the contract specifications on TMGM's own instrument pages to show how big a lot is, what a price move is worth, and how rebates are counted by lot.

How big is 1 lot at TMGM?

One standard lot of gold (XAUUSD) is 100 troy ounces, so a USD 1 move in the gold price is USD 100 per lot. One lot of EURUSD is EUR 100,000, and a 1-pip move (0.0001) is USD 10.

Instrument1 standard lotPrice moveProfit or loss per lot
Forex (EURUSD)EUR 100,0001 pip (0.0001)USD 10
Gold (XAUUSD)100 ozUSD 1.00USD 100
US oil (XTIUSD)100 barrelsUSD 0.01USD 1

The minimum trade is 0.01 lots, and profit, loss and rebates scale with the lot size.

Pip or point?

A pip is the unit traders quote; a point is the smallest price step in MT4/MT5. EURUSD is quoted to five decimals, so its point is 0.00001 and ten points make one pip.

  • MT4/MT5 show the spread in points. A EURUSD spread of 12 is 1.2 pips, about USD 12 on one lot.
  • USDJPY is quoted to three decimals: the pip is 0.01 and the point is 0.001.
  • For gold, TMGM lists the pip as 0.01, worth USD 1 per lot. Other sources sometimes call a different step a pip, so confirm the unit first.
  • The index rebate table uses its own unit: a point there is 0.01 of an index point.

For why the spread suddenly grows around opens and news, see why forex spreads widen.

How to calculate pip value

Pip value = contract size × price move × lots. The result is in the quote currency, the second currency of the pair. If that is not your account currency, convert it.

  • EURUSD: 100,000 × 0.0001 × 1 lot = USD 10.
  • Gold: 100 oz × USD 1 × 1 lot = USD 100.
  • Oil: 100 barrels × USD 0.01 × 1 lot = USD 1.
  • USDJPY: 100,000 × 0.01 × 1 lot = JPY 1,000. TMGM's example is USD 6.25 at a rate of 160.00; the USD value changes with the rate.

Contract value and margin

You do not pay the full contract value to open a lot. Your account needs margin: lots × contract size × price ÷ leverage. The examples assume 1:100 leverage; your actual leverage depends on your account and platform rules.

ExampleContract valueMargin at 1:100
EURUSD 1 lot at 1.10EUR 100,000 ≈ USD 110,000About USD 1,100
Gold 0.1 lots at USD 4,00010 oz × 4,000 = USD 40,000About USD 400

Margin is the deposit needed to open, not the most you can lose. Losses follow the pip values above and can exceed the margin used. Platforms may cut leverage before major news or market closes, which raises the margin for the same position.

How rebates follow lots

Rebates are paid on eligible closed standard lots: 0.1 lots counts as 0.1, and 0.01 lots as 0.01. With a Max Rebate TMGM Standard (STD) account:

  • Gold pays USD 20 per lot, so 0.1 lots earns USD 2 and 0.01 lots earns USD 0.20.
  • Forex pays USD 10 per lot, so 0.5 lots of EURUSD earns USD 5.
  • Closing 1.5 lots of gold and 2 lots of forex in a day earns 1.5 × 20 + 2 × 10 = USD 50. Rebates are settled daily and paid to the trading account the next day.

A lot of gold and a lot of forex are different positions with different spreads, so their per-lot rebates are not directly comparable. See the rebate and IB guide for eligibility, and forex trading costs for comparing the full cost.

Check the specification in MT4/MT5

On desktop MT4/MT5, right-click a symbol in Market Watch and choose Specification. It shows the contract size, minimum lot and number of quote digits. Specifications can differ between accounts and servers, so check the symbol in your own account before trading a new instrument and sizing a position.

Frequently asked questions

How many ounces is 1 lot of gold at TMGM?

One standard lot of XAUUSD is 100 troy ounces, 0.1 lots is 10 ounces and 0.01 lots is 1 ounce. A USD 1 move in the gold price is USD 100 per standard lot.

How much is 0.01 lots of gold per USD 1 move?

USD 1. A 0.01-lot gold position is 1 ounce, so each USD 1 change in the price is a USD 1 profit or loss, before spread, commission and swap.

Do I need USD 100,000 to trade 1 lot of forex?

No. The contract is 100,000 units of the base currency, but opening it needs only margin: contract value ÷ leverage. EURUSD at 1.10 with 1:100 leverage needs about USD 1,100. Your account's leverage applies.

What is a spread of 12 in MT4/MT5 worth?

On a five-decimal pair such as EURUSD, 12 is 12 points, which is 1.2 pips. At USD 10 per pip on one lot, that is about USD 12.

What does "100 points" mean in the index rebate table?

An index rebate point is 0.01 of an index point, so 100 points is one index point. TMGM's US30 contract is USD 10 per index point, so 100 points is about USD 10 per lot.
