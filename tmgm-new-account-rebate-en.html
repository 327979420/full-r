Just opened a TMGM account? Apply for rebates within 3 days

Registering on TMGM's own website without an invitation code or agent link does not mean you have missed out on rebates. Within 3 days of registering, your registered email is all you need to apply.

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Submit your registered email中文版
Only one email address: no trading account number, password, verification code or ID document is needed. Your registered email is used only to process this application and tell you the result: no spam and no sales pressure. Approval remains subject to platform review.

The short answer

If you registered your TMGM account within the last 3 days and did not use another agent's link or invitation code, you only need to submit your registered email to apply for rebates. No account number, password or ID is needed, and approval is subject to platform review. Accounts older than 3 days can submit their email too, and we will reply by email with your options.

Who can apply directly

Registered within 3 daysCounted from the moment you completed your TMGM registration.
No other agent's linkYou registered on TMGM's own website without someone else's invitation code or account-opening link.
Reachable by emailWe send the result to the email you used to register with TMGM.

Three steps

  1. Find the email address you used to register with TMGM.
  2. Open the application form and enter that email. From this page, “Rebate application” and “registered within 3 days: yes” are already selected.
  3. Submit, keep the reference number and watch for our email. Once access is confirmed, trade as usual; you do not need to change accounts or habits.

The form never asks for passwords, verification codes or card details. Do not give them to anyone who asks.

What happens after you submit

We use your registered email to ask the platform to place your account under the Max Rebate rebate arrangement, and we email you the result. Rebates are calculated on eligible trades after access is confirmed, so do not treat rebates as active until you hear from us.

Current rates are in the TMGM rebate table, and how rebates work is explained in the rebate guide.

Registered more than 3 days ago, or used another agent's link

Registered more than 3 days ago

Submit your registered email anyway and answer “No” to “Was the account registered within 3 days?”. We will reply by email with how your account can be handled.

Registered with another agent's link or invitation code

That account already belongs to the other agent, so it cannot be moved this way. See existing TMGM accounts for background.

Frequently asked questions

I just registered with TMGM. Can I still get rebates?

Yes. If you registered within the last 3 days and did not use another agent's link or invitation code, submit your registered email to apply. Approval is subject to platform review.

I forgot to enter an invitation code when registering. What now?

Within 3 days of registering, simply submit your registered email; you do not need to register again. If more than 3 days have passed, submit your email anyway and we will reply by email with your options.

What do I need to provide?

Only the email address you used to register with TMGM. No trading account number, password, verification code, card details or ID images.

I registered through another agent's link. Can I apply?

No. Accounts opened through another agent's link or invitation code already belong to that agent and cannot be moved this way.

When does the 3-day window start?

From the moment you complete your TMGM registration. Submit within 3 days to apply directly.

Will I get spam or sales messages after submitting?

No. Your registered email is used only to process this application and tell you the result.
